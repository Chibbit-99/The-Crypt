Classroom discovery

Find resources attached to your Google Classroom courses and create a searchable JSON manifest in your Google Drive.

Discover resources

Scans courses, assignments, class materials and announcements you’re allowed to access. Drive files are listed; external links, Forms and YouTube videos are recorded as references.

Note: This step only creates a manifest. It does not download files or upload anything to GitHub.
