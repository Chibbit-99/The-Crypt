What gets exported?

Regular downloadable Drive files are copied as-is. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are converted to DOCX, XLSX and PPTX; Drawings become PDFs. Files are deduplicated by Drive ID.

External websites, YouTube links, Forms, unsupported Google file types, inaccessible files and files excluded by the size safety limit are listed in EXPORT-REPORT.txt inside the ZIP. The ZIP is kept to a conservative size so it can be transferred through the web app.

This app only exports content your account can access. Keep the ZIP private if it contains school materials. Your school administrator may restrict Classroom or Drive access.
